Behind the Lens

I'm Omkar Shinde, a Video Editor and Videographer passionate about transforming ideas into engaging visual stories.

From capturing moments to crafting cinematic edits, I focus on creating meaningful visual experiences through editing, cinematography, storytelling and creative direction.

  • Story before shotsEvery project starts with what the viewer should feel. Framing, pacing and music follow from that.
  • Shoot for the editI film with the final cut in mind, so coverage, movement and light already serve the sequence.
  • Finish with intentColour, sound and motion graphics are the last pass that turns a clean cut into a film.
